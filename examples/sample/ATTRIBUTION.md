## Changes made

- Only PDF pages 17–49 of the 1,420-page book are included: Chapter 1 (printed pages 7–39).
- The line "Download for free at https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology."
  was added at the foot of every page, as the book's license page requires for any
  redistributed part.
- Images were downsampled to 150 dpi (JPEG quality 80) to keep the file small.
- The PDF outline was kept (the chapter and its sections); printed page numbers were
  restored as PDF page labels (7–39); the document metadata names the source and license.

The text itself is unchanged.

## Why the first edition

The current second edition (*Anatomy and Physiology 2e*, 2022) is licensed
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as of its current PDF (©2026 Rice University). The first edition — now
retired by OpenStax, still downloadable — is CC BY 4.0, which matches this repository's
MIT license without a non-commercial or share-alike condition.

## Not covered by the license

The OpenStax name, logo and book covers, and the Rice University name and logo, are not
subject to the Creative Commons license (see the book's copyright page). None of them are
reproduced here beyond the attribution line above. Photographs in the chapter carry their
own credit lines (e.g. "credit: Bryan Mason/flickr"), kept as printed.
